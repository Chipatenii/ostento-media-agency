Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on this site, grouped by category, and how you can change your choice at any time.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a website work and remember your preferences. Some are essential; others are optional and only used with your consent.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Needed for the site to function, including remembering your cookie choice. These are always on and cannot be switched off.

Analytics (optional)

Help us understand which pages are useful so we can improve them. Anonymous and aggregated. Off until you accept.

Marketing (optional)

Used to measure campaigns and show relevant content. Off until you accept.

3. Your choice

On your first visit, choose "Accept all", "Reject all" or "Manage preferences". These controls save your category preferences in this browser. External services, including the client review widget, manage their own cookies and privacy practices. You can change your choice at any time using the link, also available in the footer.

4. How we remember it

Your choice is stored locally in your browser so we do not have to ask every visit. If our cookie use materially changes, we will ask again.
