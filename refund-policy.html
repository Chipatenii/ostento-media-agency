Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This page is a starting template written in plain language, not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer (and, for EU exposure, someone familiar with GDPR) review it before launch.

We want you to feel confident starting a project with us. This policy sets out, in plain language, how deposits and milestone payments work and what is or is not refundable. It is meant to be fair to both sides.

1. Deposits

A deposit reserves your project in our schedule and covers the discovery and early design work. Once that work has started, the deposit is non-refundable, because the time has already been spent on your project.

2. Milestone payments

Larger projects are billed in milestones. Each milestone pays for work already delivered and approved. Completed and approved milestones are not refundable.

3. If you cancel

You can stop a project at any time. You pay for the work completed up to that point, and we hand over what has been produced and paid for. Any amount paid in advance of work not yet done is refunded.

4. If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to complete a project we have started, we refund any payment that covers work we have not delivered.

5. Digital work

Because design and development are custom, time-based services, we do not offer refunds simply because you change your mind about a completed, approved deliverable. If something we delivered does not match the agreed brief, tell us and we will put it right.

6. How to request a refund

Email info@ostentomediaagency.com with your project name and the details. We aim to respond within a few business days.
