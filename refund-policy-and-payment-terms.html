Refund Policy and Payment Terms

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy sets out payment terms and refund conditions for all services provided by Ostento Media Agency (a trading name of Strataforge Technologies), covering Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, Standard Website Development, and Software Development. Terms are confirmed in a signed proposal, quote, or Service Agreement before any payment is made, and that document takes precedence where it states a different amount or schedule for a specific project.

1. Graphic Design Services

A 70% deposit of the total quote value is required before work begins, covering concept development, creative direction, and scheduling of the project. The remaining 30% is due on delivery of final files.

If the client cancels before work begins: Ostento retains 30% of the total quote value as a non-refundable fee covering contract administration, scheduling, and creative planning already undertaken. The remaining portion of the deposit, equal to 40% of the total quote value, is refunded to the client.

2. Digital Marketing Services

Payment for digital marketing services is required in full upon execution of the contract, before work commences.

If the client cancels before work commences: Ostento retains 30% of the total contract value as a non-refundable onboarding and account setup fee, covering strategy development, account provisioning, campaign planning, and resource allocation already completed. The remaining 70% is refunded to the client.

3. Standard Website Development

A 70% deposit of the total invoice value is required before work begins, covering project setup, planning, and resource allocation. The remaining 30% is due on delivery.

If the client cancels before work begins: Ostento retains 30% of the total invoice value as a non-refundable fee covering project setup, planning, and resource allocation already undertaken. The remaining portion of the deposit, equal to 40% of the total invoice value, is refunded to the client.

Third-party costs such as domain registration and software licenses are addressed separately in Section 6.

4. Software Development

Payment is structured in three milestones tied to project deliverables:

Software development payment milestones
MilestonePayment
Project commencement (requirements, architecture, project setup)40%
Mid-project delivery (agreed development milestone reached)40%
Final delivery and handover20%

If the client cancels mid-project: Ostento retains the first 40% deposit in full, as it is a non-refundable fee covering requirements gathering, architecture and planning, and resources already committed in preparing and beginning the project. Any amount paid beyond the first deposit is refunded in proportion to work not yet delivered.

5. Non-Performance by Ostento Media

If Ostento Media fails to deliver agreed work, materially misses an agreed deadline, or the client terminates the contract due to Ostento's non-performance, the client is entitled to a refund based on the value of work not yet delivered. The value of work already completed and delivered, including approved milestones and drafts, is retained by Ostento. This applies across all service lines above and overrides the cancellation terms where the cause of termination is Ostento's non-performance rather than the client's choice to cancel.

6. Third-Party Costs

Costs for third-party items purchased or licensed on the client's behalf, including domain registration, hosting, SSL certificates, stock assets, and software licenses, are billed at cost and are non-refundable once purchased, regardless of project outcome or cancellation. These costs are itemized and disclosed to the client before purchase.

7. How to Request a Refund

  1. Client emails refund@ostentomedia-agency.com stating the reason for the refund request.
  2. Ostento reviews the request against the applicable terms above and confirms the outcome to the client in writing.
  3. Once approved, the refund is processed within 7 working days from the date of approval, paid back by bank transfer, mobile money, or the method the client originally used to pay.

8. General Terms

This policy is governed by the laws of Zambia, including the Competition and Consumer Protection Act No. 24 of 2010. In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to attempt resolution through direct negotiation first. Unresolved disputes may be referred to the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) or the courts of Zambia.

If any provision of this policy is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
