01 Brief 02 Plan 03 Create & review 04 Deliver

01Agree the brief

Tell us whether you need a website or web app, a business system, a marketing campaign, brand materials or large format prints. We confirm the requirements and the deliverables before quoting.

  • Your service, audience and goals
  • Required features, content or print specifications
  • Existing files, references and preferred timing

You receive: A brief with the requirements and scope recorded.

02Plan the work

We set out the costs, project stages and timing in a proposal. The plan includes the content, access and approvals needed from you, so both sides know what happens next.

  • Website pages, software workflows or campaign channels
  • Design direction and artwork requirements
  • Milestones, feedback points and delivery dates

You receive: A proposal covering the quote, milestones and responsibilities.

03Create and review

We share work at the agreed milestones. You review the previews or proofs, and we make revisions within the project scope before preparing the final work.

  • Website or software previews
  • Campaign content and brand design drafts
  • Artwork proofs for printed materials

You receive: Previews or proofs ready for your feedback and approval.

04Check, deliver and hand over

The final checks depend on your service. We test digital features, review campaign materials, prepare design files or check print dimensions and finish. Ongoing support or management is defined in your proposal.

  • Website or software testing, deployment and handover
  • Approved campaign content and brand files
  • Finished prints in the agreed sizes and quantities

You receive: The approved deliverables, with any agreed handover or next steps.
