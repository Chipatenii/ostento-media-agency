Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy describes project enquiries, browser preferences and external services used by the Ostento Media Agency website. Contact us at the address below with questions about your information.

The data controller for this website operates as Ostento Media Agency, a registered trading name in Zambia under PACRA No. 320230069962, based in Lusaka. For any privacy question or request, email growth@ostentomedia-agency.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Project enquiries: the name, contact details, selected service and project information you choose to send to us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Browser preferences: your cookie choices are saved in local storage on your device.
  • Cookies: see our Cookie Policy for the categories we use and how consent works.

2. Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to deliver the project (contract / legitimate interest).
  • To remember the browser preferences you choose on this site.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations (legal obligation).

3. Where it is stored

The contact form prepares a draft in your email app or WhatsApp. It does not submit a project enquiry to a website database. When you send the draft, your information is handled by your chosen provider and received through our business communication channels. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; fonts and the client review widget are provided by external services. Those services may receive connection information, such as your IP address, when they load.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to respond and, where relevant, for the life of the project plus any period required for legal or accounting reasons. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you can request to access, correct, delete, or port your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email growth@ostentomedia-agency.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers used for hosting, email, messaging, fonts and client reviews, each bound to protect it, and where the law requires.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and sensible safeguards to protect your data. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to keep the risk low.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email growth@ostentomedia-agency.com.
